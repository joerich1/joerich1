# Joseph Richardson

I study computer vision and diffusion models as a PhD candidate in Data Science at Kennesaw State. I used to be a graphic designer, so good design goes into every figure I make: In papers, explainers, and interactive visuals built with JavaScript.

My research asks what AI models remember about the data they were trained on. For medical imaging, the answer matters: A hospital can release a model's weights and still leak which patients it learned from. I also apply network analysis to movie data, mapping who works with whom among cast and crew.

The two visuals I'm proudest of are on [josephrichardson.dev](https://josephrichardson.dev):

- [A star graph of Samuel L. Jackson's career](https://josephrichardson.dev/essays/good-node-hunting/) that you can explore yourself.
- [An interactive explainer of the diffusion research](https://josephrichardson.dev/work/how-diffusion-models-memorize/), trained on photographs I shot on the set of an independent film. You watch the model learn them, then run the attack that finds them.

M.S. in Analytics from Georgia Tech. Four papers accepted in 2026.

## Public repos

- [richardson-index](https://github.com/joerich1/richardson-index): Compares leagues that rarely play each other, like the SEC and the Big Ten. It gives the gap between two leagues with a margin of error, and tiers of teams inside each league. It also treats the schedule as a circuit to show how much each game counts. Built for the essay [SEC Versus Big Ten, Through Everyone Else](https://josephrichardson.dev/essays/sec-versus-big-ten/).
